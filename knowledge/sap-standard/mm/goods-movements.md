# Goods Movement

## Standard knowledge

SAP S/4HANA Inventory Management describes goods movement as the process for planning, entering and documenting stock movements such as goods receipts, goods issues, physical stock transfers and transfer postings. The same documentation also identifies reporting functions for material and stock data.

## Scope

This document records only the SAP Standard behavior described by the cited SAP Help source. It does not describe customer configuration or custom developments.
