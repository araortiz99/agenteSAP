# Inventory Management Basic Principles

## Standard knowledge

SAP documentation states that Inventory Management creates a document for every goods movement, providing an audit trail for transactions.

A goods movement is identified by a movement type. SAP documentation also describes stock types as a way to divide stock for different uses and states that inventory is managed in base units of measure, with conversion when another unit is entered.

## Scope

These statements describe SAP Standard concepts. They do not establish which movement types, stock types, units or configuration are used in a specific customer system.
