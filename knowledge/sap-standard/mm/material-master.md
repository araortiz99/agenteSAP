# Material Master

## Standard knowledge

SAP documentation describes the material or product master as the central source for material-specific information. Material master information is organized in different views, and some information is specific to organizational units such as sales area, plant or storage location.

The material master is used by multiple logistics functions, including purchasing, inventory management, invoice verification and production planning.

## Scope

This document records SAP Standard concepts only. It does not assert that a specific customer material, field, view or organizational assignment is configured in a particular SAP system.
