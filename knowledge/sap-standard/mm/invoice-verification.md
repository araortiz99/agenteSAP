# Logistics Invoice Verification

## Standard knowledge

SAP S/4HANA documentation describes Logistics Invoice Verification as the process for entering and checking supplier invoices. An invoice can be allocated using references such as a purchase order, delivery note, service entry sheet or supplier.

When invoice values differ from expected values, SAP can evaluate the variance against configured tolerance limits. Depending on the result, the invoice can be processed or blocked for payment.

## Functional boundary

This document establishes SAP Standard process concepts only.

It does not establish:

- the invoice verification configuration of a customer system;
- tolerance values or blocking rules configured locally;
- customer-specific MIRO, Fiori or Z-process behavior;
- the accounting documents generated in a specific system.
